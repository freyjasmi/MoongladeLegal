Moonglade

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Moonglade grew from a love of tarot, the quiet rhythms of nature, and the energies we find in the world and in ourselves. It is a place to pause, draw a card, and listen to what stirs.

It also reflects a firmly held belief: the tools we welcome into our lives should ask only for what they need. There is value in an application that stands quietly on its own, without another account to create, a profile to build, or a wider pool of data to feed. Your moments of reflection deserve privacy.

With that in mind, this Privacy Policy explains what Moonglade: Tarot Readings ("Moonglade", "the Application") keeps about you, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

Moonglade is made by Axinqa Limited ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14826450).

In short

Your grimoire is yours. That belief guides how Moonglade is built.

  • No accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your name, questions, readings and reflections are saved in the Application's private storage on your device.
  • Your readings and your grimoire work without the internet. A connection is needed only to buy or restore purchases, which Google Play handles for us.
  • What you keep in Moonglade is never sent to us. We cannot see it.
  • Reminders are set on your phone and sent by your phone. Nothing is sent to us to make them.
  • A reading leaves your phone only if you choose to share it, and only to the place you choose (see section 6).
  • Purchases are made through Google Play. We never see your payment card or bank details (see section 9).
  • Your phone’s own backup service may copy the Application’s data to your Google account. Google runs that backup, and we cannot access it (see section 7).

1. Who we are

Moonglade is made and published by Axinqa Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14826450). If you have a question about this policy or about your information, please write to devfreyish@gmail.com.

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR, we are the data controller for the limited personal data we do handle, which is described in sections 8 to 10.

2. What Moonglade keeps on your phone

Moonglade is designed to be your personal grimoire: a place for the cards you draw, the questions you bring, and the meanings you discover along the way. To keep those pages for you, it saves the following in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Your name, if you choose to give one, and the familiar you choose
  • The date you first opened the Application
  • The readings you choose to save: the cards drawn, whether each was reversed, the deck, the date, the question you wrote (if you wrote one) and any reflection you write about it
  • Your tea leaf readings, if you save them: the symbols found and where they lay in the cup
  • Today's daily reading, the current Moonglade Reading, and any other reading you have begun but not finished, so that you can come back to them and so that the same cards cannot be drawn twice
  • The cards you have discovered in each deck
  • How many of the week's free readings you have used
  • Your settings: the deck in use, music, sound effects, the reading ritual, the tea leaves introduction, shuffle and cut, reversals, and your reminders with the times you chose for them
  • Whether you have seen the welcome and the tour, and whether the Application has already asked you for a review
  • What you have bought, as confirmed by Google Play: the decks you own, and whether you have full access, by subscription or Lifetime access

Other apps on your phone cannot read this storage. Readings you discard are not kept in your grimoire, although the cards in them still count as discovered.

3. Your questions and reflections

Some questions are easy to speak aloud. Others need a quieter place.

What you write in Moonglade may touch on your health, your relationships, your beliefs or other deeply personal matters. Your questions and reflections are saved on your phone, subject to the phone backups described in section 7. We never receive them, never read them, and cannot share them with anyone because we do not hold them.

Please remember that anyone who can unlock your phone can open Moonglade and read what you have kept in it.

4. Offline by design, and no tracking

Moonglade is deliberately built to work offline. We believe a tool for reflection should leave you room to be still, without gathering information about you in the background.

Your readings, your grimoire and everything you write in them work without the internet. The Application contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies and no third-party code that collects information about you. There is no Moonglade account to create, and no account connection is required to use its readings or grimoire.

The Application uses the internet for one purpose only: purchases. When you buy a deck, a subscription or Lifetime access, or restore a purchase on a new phone, Moonglade asks Google Play to carry it out, and Google Play connects to Google's servers to do so. These connections carry only what Google needs to complete the purchase. Nothing you have kept in Moonglade, such as your name, questions, readings or reflections, is ever sent.

A few things you may choose to do hand you over to another app on your phone: opening a link (such as this policy on the web) opens your web browser, "Write a Review" opens Moonglade's page in Google Play, and sharing a reading opens your phone's share sheet. Those apps, and the places you share to, work under their own privacy policies.

Android lists the permissions Moonglade has on its page in Google Play. They allow the Application to make and confirm purchases through Google Play; to show the reminders you turn on, and to set them again after your phone restarts; to make the small vibrations you feel as cards are turned; and to let the audio library it uses for its music check whether the phone has a connection. None of them is used to send what you keep in Moonglade anywhere.

5. Reminders

If you turn on a reminder in Details, for your daily reading or for each new phase of the moon, Moonglade asks your phone to show it at the times you chose. To time them correctly, it reads your phone's time zone, and it works out the moon's phases on the phone itself. Reminders are scheduled and shown by your phone alone: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn them off at any time in Details or in your phone's settings.

6. Sharing a reading

You can share a reading as a picture. Moonglade makes the picture on your phone and shows it to you first, and it leaves your question out unless you choose to include it. Your phone's share sheet then sends it, with a line naming Moonglade and a link to it on Google Play, only to the app or person you choose. The picture is kept in the Application's temporary storage on your phone until your phone clears it. We never receive it. Once shared, it is held by the app you sent it to, under that app's own terms.

7. Backups made by your phone

A grimoire can gather meaning over time. Moonglade allows Android’s backup feature so that yours can be restored if you reinstall the Application or move to a new phone. This means Android may copy the Application's data to your Google account. These backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account, not by us, and we cannot access them. They are covered by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can turn backup off in your phone's settings (often under Settings, Google, Backup, though this varies from phone to phone), and you can delete backups from your Google account.

8. Google Play

Moonglade is downloaded from Google Play, which Google runs under its own privacy policy.

If you have chosen, in your phone's settings, to share usage and diagnostics information with app developers, Google may send us reports of crashes and performance problems through the Google Play Console. These reports describe the phone and the fault, such as the phone's model and its version of Android. They do not identify you, and they never include your readings or anything you have written in the Application.

If you choose to rate or review Moonglade, whether on the card Google Play may show you in the Application or on Moonglade's page in Google Play, Google publishes your review under your Google Play name, under its own terms. We see it in the Google Play Console, as anyone can on Google Play, and we may reply to it there. The Application itself never learns whether, or what, you wrote.

9. Purchases and subscriptions

Moonglade offers optional purchases: full access to the Application, either as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a single payment for Lifetime access, and additional decks, each bought once. Prices and how each purchase works are set out in our Terms of Use. All of them are sold and processed by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. You pay Google, using the payment method saved in your Google account, and we never see your payment card or bank details.

When you buy something, Google gives us limited details of the order, such as an order number, the item, the price, the date, the country of purchase and, for a subscription, whether it is active, renewed or cancelled. We use these details only to provide what you bought, to restore it to you, to handle refunds and questions about your order, and to keep the accounting and tax records the law requires.

To know what you have bought, the Application asks Google Play on your phone and keeps the answer in its private storage (see section 2). It does not send that answer to us: Moonglade has no server of its own. Your purchases are linked to your Google account, not to a Moonglade account, which is why they can be restored on another phone signed in to the same Google account.

10. If you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply to you and to help you, and we delete the correspondence once the matter is settled, unless we need to keep it to meet a legal obligation.

11. Our legal bases

Information kept only on your phone is not collected by us, so we do not process it. For the information we do handle, our legal bases are:

  • Order details from Google Play: to perform our contract with you (providing and restoring what you bought), and to meet our legal obligations to keep accounting and tax records.
  • Emails you send us: our legitimate interest in answering you and helping you.
  • Crash and performance reports: our legitimate interest in finding and fixing faults in the Application.
  • Reviews on Google Play: our legitimate interest in hearing what people think of Moonglade, and in replying.

12. Who else handles this information

We do not sell personal data, and we share it with no one for advertising. The information we handle is held for us by Google, which provides Google Play, the Google Play Console and our email. Google may store and process it outside the UK and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where it does, the transfer is protected by the safeguards the law requires, such as an adequacy decision or the standard data protection clauses approved for the UK and the EU. We may also share order details with our accountants, and with tax authorities when the law requires it.

13. How long information is kept

Moonglade's data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a saved reading from Reading History, or remove everything at once by clearing Moonglade's storage in your phone's settings (often under Settings, Apps, Moonglade, Storage) or by uninstalling the Application. A copy may remain in your Google backup until you delete it there or it expires. Your purchases are not lost when you do this: they stay with your Google account and can be restored.

We keep order details for as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually six years after the end of the financial year in which the purchase was made. We keep emails only as long as section 10 describes. Crash reports are kept by Google for the period it sets.

14. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable form.

Because nothing you keep in the Application reaches us, you can see, change and delete all of it yourself, on your phone. For anything we do hold, such as your order details or your emails to us, write to devfreyish@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. Some order details must be kept for the period the law requires, even if you ask us to delete them.

Where we rely on our legitimate interests, you can object, and we will stop unless we have compelling reasons to continue.

15. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us by writing to devfreyish@gmail.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it without undue delay, and tell you what we found and what we have done.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or, if you live in the European Union, to the data protection authority in your country.

16. Children

Moonglade is meant for people aged 16 and over, and is not directed at children. The Application sends us nothing from what you keep in it, so we do not collect information from anyone who uses it. Purchases are made through a Google account, under Google's rules for younger users. If a child has bought something or emailed us, a parent or guardian can contact us about it.

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will also say so in the Application's release notes on Google Play.
